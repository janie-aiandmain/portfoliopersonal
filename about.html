Services leadership grounded in how people actually adopt technology.

I started in the classroom, spent years leading customer-facing SaaS services, and now also see K–12 from the district-governance side. That combination shapes how I think about adoption: it is not a training event. It is an operating system.

Professional background & philosophy

Build the system around the outcome.

My work has ranged from classroom instruction and coaching to global customer education, specialized professional-services teams, post-acquisition service integration, and district governance.

I am most useful when the problem crosses functions: customers are buying services but not using the product deeply enough; Sales needs a faster way to quote without pulling Services into every call; Support sees recurring friction that Services can prevent; Product needs structured field insight; or a team is spending skilled human time on work that should be standardized or automated.

The point of services is not activity. It is durable customer behavior and measurable value.
Operating approach

Diagnose → Design → Operationalize → Measure → Improve

01Diagnose

Find friction in customer behavior, delivery, data, and handoffs.

02Design

Define the service model, experience, roles, and success signals.

03Operationalize

Build repeatable workflows, tools, standards, and ownership.

04Measure

Track adoption, service quality, support demand, margin, and repurchase.

05Improve

Feed field insight back into Product, Support, Marketing, and Services.
