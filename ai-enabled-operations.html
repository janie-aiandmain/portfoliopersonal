AI & Automation as Operating Leverage

I use automation where it can remove repeatable administrative work and AI where it can accelerate synthesis or production—while keeping judgment, relationship, and accountability with people.

AIWorkflow AutomationProfessional ServicesHuman-in-the-Loop
25%lower service scheduling/prep effort

My operating principle

AI adoption should start with the work, not the tool. I first identify where skilled employees are spending time on routing, repetition, synthesis, formatting, or administrative preparation. Then I decide what can be standardized, automated, or AI-assisted—and what should remain explicitly human.

Examples

Service coordination

Used structured packages, Salesforce workflow, Zapier automation, and reusable facilitator resources to reduce scheduling and session-preparation effort.

Course production

In current instructional-design work, use AI-assisted development and reusable templates to move from SME input to accessible learning experiences more efficiently.

Knowledge reuse

Turn recurring customer needs, support themes, and facilitator insight into reusable guidance rather than solving the same problem from scratch.

Guardrails

Keep client-specific recommendations, sensitive decisions, and final quality review with accountable human owners.
