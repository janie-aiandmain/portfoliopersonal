Elected public service

District-side perspective on strategy, governance & technology adoption.

Serving as the elected School Board Member for Flagler County Schools, District 3, gives me direct exposure to the decisions districts make about priorities, budgets, policy, implementation, stakeholder trust, and long-term student outcomes.

Board leadership & impact

I provide governance oversight related to district strategy, fiscal accountability, instructional quality, technology adoption, workforce readiness, and policy. That perspective informs how I think about the responsibilities EdTech partners carry after a contract is signed.

District leaders are balancing competing priorities, public accountability, staff capacity, funding constraints, and implementation realities. Services succeed when they respect that context.
