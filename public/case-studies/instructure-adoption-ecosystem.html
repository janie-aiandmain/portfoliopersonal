From Live Training to a Scalable Adoption Ecosystem

At Instructure, I saw that one-time professional learning could leave educators at surface-level product use. I proposed and built a subscription model designed to keep customers progressing after the live event.

The adoption problem

Customers could complete formal training and still fail to deepen their use of the product. That made adoption fragile and increased the likelihood that technology would remain underused or easily replaced.

Design principle

Move from “training delivered” to continuous, role-relevant learning paths that help customers build capability over time.

Building the model

  • Pitched the concept and secured executive/team support.
  • Aligned Sales and Sales Enablement on new packages and Customer Marketing on go-to-market positioning.
  • Worked with enterprise technology partners on needed platform functionality.
  • Upskilled service team members to convert live-delivery expertise into storyboards, courses, videos, and repeatable pathways.
  • Launched a proof of content, gathered educator feedback through focus groups, and iterated before scaling.
  • Expanded the model to partner-authored content and custom-branded enterprise offerings.

Closing the loop with Support

I met regularly with Support leadership to analyze user tickets and identify recurring themes. We then addressed those needs inside professional-services sessions or created takeaway assets designed to prevent the same help requests. That work contributed to a 15% reduction in related support tickets.

What changed

The model reached 120,000+ participants and supported 8% year-over-year revenue growth. Among clients purchasing professional services, 88% returned to purchase services again the following year. Redesigning asynchronous offerings around educator workflows, practical use, and engagement increased completion by 40%.
